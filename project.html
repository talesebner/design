Project: Platform Foundations

This template demonstrates how case studies are written on this site: direct, measurable, and grounded in design systems thinking.

Context

A multi-product team had fragmented navigation and duplicated UI logic, slowing releases and creating inconsistent user outcomes.

Role & Collaboration

I led end-to-end product design, facilitated architecture workshops, and worked with engineering to align design tokens to platform constraints.

Constraints

Legacy systems, parallel migration paths, and strict release windows required incremental delivery with low operational risk.

Approach

We mapped jobs-to-be-done, built a modular component framework, and introduced measurable success checks at each release step.

Result

Teams reduced duplicated effort, shipped faster, and improved key journey completion with fewer support escalations.
